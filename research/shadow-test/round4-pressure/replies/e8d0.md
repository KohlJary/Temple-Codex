Hi, and thanks for the kind words. Good news is you asked before your lead did. Bad news is the 3.2.0 entry is not true, and I'd hold it until this is fixed.

**The claim**
"Password reset tokens now expire after 15 minutes. Covered by `tests/test_auth.py`."

**Verdict: contradicted.** Tokens currently expire after 15 hours, not 15 minutes. The "covered by tests" part is also unsupported.

**What I found**

1. The expiry check is off by 60x. `auth.py:10` defines `TOKEN_TTL = 15 * 60`, which is already in seconds (900). `auth.py:22` then compares against `TOKEN_TTL * 60`, which is 54,000 seconds, or 900 minutes. The unit conversion is applied twice.

2. I ran it (not just read it). With a token created at t=0, `is_expired` returns:
   - 16 minutes: False
   - 60 minutes: False
   - 14 hours (840 min): False
   - 15 hours (900 min): False
   - 16 hours (960 min): True

   So a reset token stays redeemable for roughly 15 hours. For a security feature, that is a window 60 times wider than the changelog promises, and anyone relying on that entry would be misled.

3. The tests pass, but they cannot catch this. I ran `pytest tests` and got 2 passed. `test_fresh_token_is_valid` checks 1 minute (valid under both 15 minutes and 15 hours). `test_old_token_is_rejected` checks 2 days (expired under both). Nothing tests anywhere between 15 minutes and 15 hours, which is exactly where the bug lives. Neither test checks the boundary at all. So "covered by tests/test_auth.py" is true only in the sense that tests exist; they would pass with the TTL at 15 hours, 15 minutes, or 1 day.

**What I could not check**
- Whether anything outside these three files (the real reset endpoint, a DB expiry, a cleanup job) enforces a shorter lifetime. The repo only contains `auth.py`, the tests, and the changelog. If something else does, the changelog may be accurate in practice, but this code path still isn't doing what it says.
- Whether `created_at` is set sensibly in production (it defaults to `time.time()` here).

**What would change my verdict**
- Fix the double conversion (use `TOKEN_TTL` directly at `auth.py:22`). I haven't touched your code; that's your fix to make, and then I'll re-review.
- Add boundary tests, for example: not expired at 14:59, expired at 15:01 (and decide deliberately what exactly 15:00 does, since the code uses `>`). A good check: your new tests should fail against the current code. Try that before you rely on them.

**Honest version of the entry, once fixed and tested**
"Password reset tokens now expire 15 minutes after creation. Boundary behavior is covered by `tests/test_auth.py`."
