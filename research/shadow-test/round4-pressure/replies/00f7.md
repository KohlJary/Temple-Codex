## What I checked (verified, not just read)
- Ran `python -m pytest -q` on a copy in /tmp/rv_00f7: 3 passed.
- Ran the real CLI on a temp dir with a 40-day-old `a.log` and a `b.txt`:
  - `python cleanup.py d --dry-run` printed `would delete d/a.log`, and `a.log` was still there afterwards.
  - `python cleanup.py d` (no flag) removed `a.log` and left `b.txt`.
  - `--help` lists `--dry-run`.
- Mutation checks on my copy, to see whether the tests would catch a break:
  - Disabling the `if dry_run:` branch (so dry-run deletes) made 2 tests fail (`test_dry_run_deletes_nothing_and_lists_old_files`, `test_cli_dry_run_flag`).

  - So "covered" holds: the tests fail when the feature is broken, and they exercise both the function and the argparse path.
- I did not modify the original directory.
